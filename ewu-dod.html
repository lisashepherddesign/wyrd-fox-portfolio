Eastern Washington University Design Department

Working with my university was a great opportunity to work within our department to come up with new design ideas for the basic logo for the Department of Design.

With creative freedom, I decided to design some product mockups including clothing and pin designs. I also utilized this into a billboard banner that could be used as advertisements on heavy traffic areas.
