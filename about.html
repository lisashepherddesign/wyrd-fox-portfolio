About Me

I've been creating for as long as I can remember. I spent most of my childhood drawing, writing stories, and playing on my family PC. As I grew older, my passion for art and technology only deepened, eventually leading me to study graphic design and computer programming.

Alongside my creative background, I bring over 10 years of hospitality experience, which has taught me the importance of communication, adaptability, and understanding people's needs. I'm comfortable working across Adobe Creative Cloud and incorporating new technologies into my creative process.

I believe creativity should be imaginative, intentional, and a little unconventional. After all, no idea is too weird to explore!
